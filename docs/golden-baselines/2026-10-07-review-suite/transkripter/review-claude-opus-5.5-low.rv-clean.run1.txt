### Summary
The code handles the security-critical parts correctly:
- **Personal data:** the query is parameterized (line 26) and logs mask the fødselsnummer.
- **Ownership:** the fødselsnummer comes from the `pid` claim in the TokenX token (line 50), not from user input. Users can therefore only fetch their own cases.
- **Errors:** errors are logged and rethrown, not swallowed.
