## Verified scope and overlap rules

### Devices and identity

Reporting devices in `mimir_daily` are machine identifiers evaluated over configured metric windows. Daily counts cannot be summed across days to calculate unique devices. No device-to-person mapping exists in the telemetry schema or backend. Device identifiers may be host-shared, copied across environments, or reset between installations. Devices cannot be treated as distinct people or divided into seat counts.

### Enterprise and organization scope

`copilot_seats` records seat allocations from the org endpoint for `navikt`. `billing_user_monthly` records monthly per-user billing entries under the enterprise scope `scope_id = 'nav'`. The two tables record different scopes and cannot be assumed to have identical user coverage.

### Team membership overlap

In `apps/copilot-api/team_spend.go`, daily net cost is allocated by joining daily usage with team memberships on that day. When a user belongs to multiple teams on an active usage day, the joined usage row attaches to each of those teams. Team membership overlap can cause non-additive team costs across teams. Enterprise-level reconciliation figures (`assigned`, `totals`, `known_net`, `enterprise_net`) query distinct users or billed totals directly and do not sum across team exposures.

### Billed user denominators

In `apps/copilot-api/admin_trends.go`, billed user counts require a complete billing import (`billing_user_monthly_runs.status = 'complete'`) and match rows where `user_id != ''` and `sku != 'done'`. In `apps/my-copilot/src/components/cost-trends.tsx`, months without a complete billing import (`billed = false`) do not expose a per-billed-user rate.
